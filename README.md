# SalesPulse BI

## Sales Performance & Profitability Intelligence Model

Turn raw sales transactions into meaningful business insights using Power BI, helping businesses understand revenue, profitability, regional performance, product performance, discounts, and customer trends.

---

## Project Overview

SalesPulse BI is a data-driven Sales Analytics project designed to transform large-scale e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights.

The project analyzes sales transactions to understand what is happening in the business, where performance is strong or weak, and which factors are influencing sales and profitability.

Instead of looking at thousands of individual transactions separately, SalesPulse BI converts raw transaction data into meaningful metrics, performance analysis, patterns, and business insights

## Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every day, containing information about sales, prices, discounts, quantities, regions, categories, payments, and returns. However, raw transaction data alone does not provide a clear picture of overall business performance.
