SalesPulse BI

Sales Performance & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard
Turn raw sales transactions into meaningful business insights using Power BI, helping businesses understand revenue, profitability, regional performance, product performance, discounts, and customer trends.

Project Overview

SalesPulse BI is an interactive Business Intelligence and Sales Analytics project designed to transform large-scale e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights.
The project analyzes sales transactions to understand what is happening in the business, where performance is strong or weak, and which factors are influencing sales and profitability.
Instead of looking at thousands of individual transactions, SalesPulse BI converts the data into meaningful KPIs, performance metrics, interactive visualizations, and business insights.

Business Objective
The primary objective of SalesPulse BI is to help businesses answer important questions such as:

How much revenue is being generated?
How much profit is the business making?
Which categories generate the highest sales?
Which regions perform the best?
Where is profitability weak?
How do discounts affect sales and profit?
What is the return rate?
Which products or segments need attention?
How is sales performance changing over time?
